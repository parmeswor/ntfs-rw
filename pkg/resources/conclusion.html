NTFS RW is installed

  1. Open NTFS RW from Applications.
  2. If it asks for macFUSE / ntfs-3g, click Install… and follow the steps in Terminal.
  3. When it asks for Full Disk Access, click Open Settings and switch on NTFS RW. macOS blocks access to external disks without it.
  4. Connect your NTFS drive — it becomes writable automatically.

Command line: ntfsctl status, sudo ntfsctl mount disk4s1, sudo ntfsctl auto off, ntfsctl help.

To remove: NTFS RW → Settings → Uninstall, or sudo ntfsctl uninstall.
