NTFS RW is installed

  1. Open NTFS RW from Applications.
  2. When it asks for Full Disk Access, click Open Settings and switch on NTFS RW.
  3. If macOS says “System Extension Blocked”, click Show Me How in NTFS RW and follow the steps (Apple Silicon: one-time Recovery-mode setting; Intel: click Allow).
  4. Connect your NTFS drive — it becomes writable automatically.

Command line (optional): ntfsctl status, ntfsctl doctor, ntfsctl help.

To remove: NTFS RW → Settings → Uninstall.
