NTFS RW

Developed by Pamdai

Read and write Windows (NTFS) drives on your Mac.

This installs:

  • NTFS RW.app in Applications — see your NTFS drives, make them writable, change settings.
  • Auto-mount service — when you plug in an NTFS drive it is switched to read/write automatically.
  • ntfsctl command-line tool in /usr/local/bin.

Requirements

NTFS RW uses the free macFUSE and ntfs-3g drivers. If they are not installed yet, open NTFS RW after installation and click Install… (uses Homebrew), or run ntfsctl install-deps in Terminal.

Apple Silicon: macFUSE needs its system extension allowed once (Recovery → Startup Security Utility → Reduced Security, then approve it in System Settings → Privacy & Security).

Full Disk Access: macOS only lets apps access external disks directly with Full Disk Access. NTFS RW will guide you to switch it on for NTFS RW in System Settings → Privacy & Security.

Important: always Eject NTFS drives before unplugging, and fully shut down Windows (disable Fast Startup) before connecting a drive that Windows used.
