NTFS RW

Developed by Pamdai

Read and write Windows (NTFS) drives on your Mac. Everything needed is included — no Homebrew, no Terminal, no extra downloads.

This installs:

  • NTFS RW.app in Applications — see your NTFS drives, make them writable, change settings.
  • Auto-mount service — NTFS drives become writable automatically when you plug them in.
  • macFUSE (official, unmodified) — only if your Mac doesn't already have it.
  • ntfs-3g — the open-source NTFS driver.

After installing — two one-time permissions

  1. Full Disk Access for NTFS RW, so macOS lets it use external disks.
  2. Allow the macFUSE system extension. On Apple Silicon Macs this needs a setting in Recovery mode (about 5 minutes). Intel Macs just click Allow.

NTFS RW shows you each step when it's needed.

Important: always Eject NTFS drives before unplugging, and fully shut down Windows (disable Fast Startup) before connecting a drive that Windows used.
