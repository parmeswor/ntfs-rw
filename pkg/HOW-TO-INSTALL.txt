NTFS RW  —  Read & write NTFS (Windows) drives on your Mac
Developed by Pamdai
==========================================================

Works on macOS 13 Ventura or later, Apple Silicon and Intel Macs.


1. OPEN THE INSTALLER
   Double-click "NTFS-RW-@VERSION@.pkg".

   This installer is not signed by Apple, so macOS may say it "cannot be
   opened" or "Apple could not verify" it. To allow it (one time only):
     • Click "Done" (do NOT move it to the Bin).
     • Open System Settings -> Privacy & Security.
     • Scroll down and click "Open Anyway" next to NTFS-RW-@VERSION@.pkg,
       then enter your password.
   (macOS 14 and older: right-click the .pkg -> Open -> Open.)

   Follow the installer and click Install.


2. INSTALL THE DRIVERS (once per Mac)
   Open "NTFS RW" from Applications and click "Install…" in the blue banner.
   Terminal opens and installs macFUSE and ntfs-3g using Homebrew.
   No Homebrew? Install it first from https://brew.sh

   Apple Silicon Macs (M1/M2/M3/M4…) must also allow the macFUSE system
   extension once:
     a. Shut down. Press and hold the power button until "Loading startup
        options" appears, then choose Options -> Continue.
     b. Utilities menu -> Startup Security Utility -> select your disk ->
        Security Policy -> "Reduced Security" and tick "Allow user
        management of kernel extensions from identified developers".
     c. Restart. Open System Settings -> Privacy & Security and click
        "Allow" for the macFUSE developer (Benjamin Fleischer). Restart.
   Intel Macs: just click "Allow" in Privacy & Security if asked.


3. ALLOW FULL DISK ACCESS (once per Mac)
   In NTFS RW, click "Open Settings" in the orange banner, then turn on
   "NTFS RW" in Full Disk Access. If it isn't listed, click "+" and choose
   NTFS RW from Applications. macOS blocks access to external disks
   without this.


4. PLUG IN YOUR NTFS DRIVE
   It becomes writable automatically and you'll see a notification.
   Or click "Make Writable" in the NTFS RW window.


TIPS
  • Always Eject the drive before unplugging it.
  • Terminal commands:  ntfsctl status   |   ntfsctl doctor   |   ntfsctl help
  • Uninstall: NTFS RW -> Settings -> "Uninstall NTFS RW…"
